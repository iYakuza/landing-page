COUNT THE SIGNAL.
NOT THE PERSON.

We want visitors to see that this project is finding its way into the world. The public counters show aggregate activity, not a list of people. The work matters more than surveillance.

WHAT WE COUNT

Four numbers.
No dossiers.

Page views are recorded page loads. Anonymous sessions are temporary tab-session identifiers used to estimate visits, not identify a person. Referral counts show visits where a browser supplied another site's referrer. The seven-day count is a rolling window.

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

No IPs.
No fingerprints.

The analytics table does not store IP addresses, exact location, names, email addresses, poetry or message contents, or persistent visitor profiles. The tracker skips the CMS area and respects the browser's Do Not Track setting.

LIMITS AND HONESTY

Useful counts,
not the whole truth.

Ad blockers, privacy settings, browser referrer policies, and visitors who opt out can reduce counts. A session is not a unique human being. Counts begin only after analytics is deployed, and numbers can lag while a page view is sent.

YOUR CHOICE

The experience
comes first.

Music and visual interactions remain optional. The quiet drone is generated locally when you press its button; it is not uploaded or stored. You can explore the entire project without enabling sound.
