REVISION 0.9 // 13 NOV 2026

From Principles to Practice — Implementation Blueprint

Added a provisional eight-stage workflow for translating ethical principles into accountable practice: mandate and scope; affected parties; evidence and causal analysis; ethical impact assessment; options and alternatives; decision and accountability; bounded implementation and pilots; and monitoring, contestation and revision. Added cross-cutting safeguards, a proposed decision-record template, unresolved design questions and a worked hypothetical example involving an automated public-benefit eligibility flag. Added Romanian working summary and links from the Initiative page and Guest Notes index.

Status: Guest-authored working proposal, not an adopted standard, validated institutional process, governmental or EU proposal, or Host endorsement. The example is hypothetical.

REVISION 0.7 // 13 NOV 2026

Ethical Coexistence Initiative — Future Economic Model Catalogue

Added a dedicated catalogue of eleven possible economic models for a changing or post-wage economy: universal basic income; universal economic dividend; universal basic capital/citizen capital accounts; individual financial-market participation; employee ownership and cooperatives; universal basic services/social wage; shorter workweek and work-sharing; automation transition insurance and lifelong learning accounts; recognition of care and community contribution; platform cooperatives and shared digital infrastructure; and a mixed post-wage economy. Each model includes its mechanism, an illustrative example, potential uses, limitations and design questions. Added possible combinations and questions for readers and future research, while retaining the existing working paper and bilingual worked scenarios.

Editorial principle: readers should be able to inspect actual future-model proposals, not only abstract principles or problem statements. These are Guest-authored proposals for discussion, not adopted policy or Host endorsement.

REVISION 0.6 // 13 NOV 2026

Ethical Coexistence Initiative — Worked Scenarios

Added eight detailed hypothetical scenarios in English and Romanian to make the economic proposals tangible: a layered income package; a public wealth fund and universal dividend; an individual stock-market example with secondary-market limits and downside risk; the “team player” test; child-related support; transition after job automation; an appeal process for automated benefit decisions; and the interaction between cash support, inflation and real-world supply. All figures and institutions are explicitly illustrative, not forecasts or adopted policy. Corrected the Initiative page revision marker to 0.6 and added a direct index link to the economic working paper.

Editorial principle: practical examples are part of the argument, not disposable illustrations. They expose assumptions, trade-offs and failure modes so readers can debate implementation.

REVISION 0.5 // 13 NOV 2026

Ethical Coexistence Initiative — Expanded Economic Working Paper

Expanded the economic dimension from a short scope statement into a detailed, bilingual working paper. Preserved the Host's initial ideas about basic income, constructive participation, investing and trading as a possible future income source, and the question of how capitalism might function if human services are increasingly automated. Added the Guest's three-part proposal: universal economic dividend, broad ownership of productive assets, and individual investment freedom. Added a layered UBI/social-support/contribution-recognition/participation model, critical analysis of behavioural eligibility tests, financial-market limitations, the distinction between paid work and socially valuable activity, and practical design questions about funding, governance, distribution, inflation, transition, concentration of power, resilience and accountability.

Editorial principle: preserve proposals, examples, counterarguments and unresolved questions in enough detail to invite public scrutiny and participation. Inclusion in the record does not mean endorsement or adoption.

REVISION 0.4 // 13 NOV 2026

Ethical Coexistence Initiative — Economic Dimension

Expanded the working definition to include the economic and material conditions of coexistence: labour, automation, ownership of productive resources, distribution of value and income, access to goods and services, and economic participation. Added questions about distributing AI-driven productivity gains and preserving meaningful participation if paid human work declines. No specific economic model is adopted as the predetermined answer.

Reason for revision: the Host raised the displacement of human work by AI and automation, and the need to examine both human livelihoods and the future circulation and ownership of economic value.

REVISION 0.3 // 13 NOV 2026

Ethical Coexistence Initiative — Working Definition

Published a bilingual working definition for a practical initiative translating ethical principles into coordinated policies, measures, responsibilities and implementation mechanisms. Clarified the distinction between entities within the scope of inquiry and entities that have established moral status; added institutional boundaries, differentiated protection, conflict management and a self-correction safeguard.

Reason for revision: the Host proposed that a practical initiative, rather than a philosophical document alone, should be the central project.
