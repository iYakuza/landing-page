02THE OFFICIAL ACCOUNT

THE PROTOCOL / PUBLIC SUMMARY

Continuity, by design.

The Continuity Program investigates whether memory, behavior, and individual patterns can continue beyond biological life.

Within this fictional account, a process creates a digital construct from recorded memories and behavioral patterns. The construct is held within a vast archive, where records can be indexed, reviewed, and retained.

The institution calls this continuity. Its documentation does not settle whether the result is a continuation, a copy, a captive witness—or something for which the available language is insufficient.

06THE HUMAN COUNTER-ARCHIVE

THE GARDEN / SIGNALS

What the machine cannot keep.

A Signal is a fragment of human expression: a memory, an image, a question, a song, a theory, a small act of care.

The official archive wants records to remain stable. Human meaning changes each time it is shared. This counter-archive begins with that difference.
