# Match My Mood — Emotion Preset Catalogue

These eight profiles are predefined visual art directions based on Plutchik's primary emotions. They are not psychological rules or claims that every person with a particular feeling wants the same visual treatment. A visitor chooses the closest fit and can reset the theme at any time.

## Visual intention

- **Joy:** warmth, openness and a little room to breathe.
- **Trust:** a grounded, steady atmosphere with soft edges.
- **Fear:** quiet, spacious and clear without pretending the dark is not there.
- **Surprise:** a crisp, bright shift of perspective and unexpected energy.
- **Sadness:** a slower, gentler space that does not ask the visitor to hurry.
- **Disgust:** earthy, direct and uncluttered; a boundary made visible.
- **Anger:** strong contrast, firm structure and space for intensity.
- **Anticipation:** a thoughtful, luminous atmosphere oriented toward what may come.

## Typography system

The presets use four local font stacks:

- **Serif:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif.
- **Sans:** Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
- **Monospace:** ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace.
- **Humanist:** Trebuchet MS, Arial, sans-serif.

No profile loads a remote font or requires a third-party font service.

## Palette guardrails

- Keep normal text and controls at a 4.5:1 contrast target.
- Keep borders and non-text visual indicators at a 3:1 target where applicable.
- Do not reduce contrast just to make a mood feel muted.
- Accent colours should not replace clear focus indicators.
- Keep link states and interactive states distinguishable without relying on hue alone.
- The renderer may adjust the authored colour tokens slightly to meet the contrast safeguards.

## Design boundary

A preset changes the visual atmosphere, not the meaning or content of the site. It must not hide navigation, alter published writing, or imply that one emotional state is better than another.
