01 / The intention

Shiva is the vision.
Green Dream is its expression.

This is one room in a larger world: a creative project imagined as something Shiva is dreaming. The music is the journey; the people around it help the world grow. We move from disturbance to pattern, from many frequencies toward one, then back to silence.

01 — LISTEN

Stay curious.

Share sounds, references, fragments, strange ideas, and works-in-progress. The unfinished thing belongs here too.

02 — MAKE

Make room.

Musicians, producers, writers, visual artists, listeners, and curious minds can all bring something to the table.

03 — RETURN

Leave space.

Keep it human. Respect context and credit. No pressure to perform, no claims to have all the answers.
